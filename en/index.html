AIGC 03 / PIXEL MV

“The Chosen” · 8-bit pixel lyric video

720×960 / 19s / 14 scenes · plays inline

A pixel lyric video for SASIOVERLXRD's “The Chosen”. Nineteen seconds, eight lines, fourteen scenes — room, town, stage, hill, garage, store, tunnel, pool, shore, alley, car, hotel, kitchen, road. Red carries the whole film as its accent; the cover title sits bottom-right so it clears the highlight on the face.

The frames aren't drawn by hand: a pixel scene generator I wrote lays the timeline out against a 182 BPM grid. I checked 42 cut points against the beat — worst drift 10.8 ms, median 0.2 ms.

05 / Digital human

MemPort · Digital-human talking-head video

One selfie as the source · 1920×1080 / 49s · Voice-over + screen capture · Plays right here

No camera was ever switched on: I supplied one selfie and a script, and the lip sync, facial expression and framing were generated by a digital-human platform. Everything else was mine — script structure, screen recording, animated typography, edit, grade and both cover ratios. Three beats: open by naming the pain ("switch AI tools and you explain yourself all over again"), cut to the screen capture showing how six MCP tools store facts, preferences and projects separately, then close on "600 free calls per month." The point isn't "I can generate video with AI" — it's that a talking-head video can be produced consistently without any filming setup, which is what makes product content sustainable for a one-person team.
